[b]Rusted Stellaris[/b] brings interstellar expansion and fleet warfare to Rusted Warfare. Build your empire's economy, research technology, establish starbases, and command fleets across the galaxy.

[b]What awaits you[/b]
• Choose a conventional empire, machine intelligence, or hive mind.
• Develop energy, mineral, alloy, science, and strategic resource production.
• Build fleets from corvettes to a juggernaut and defend your territory with upgraded starbases.
• Pursue megastructures and wield colossus-scale power.
• Play across three starfield maps: Twin Chokepoints (2 players), Three Arms (4 players), and Shattered Galaxy (8 players).
• Explore with four original instrumental tracks in the mod's music rotation.

[b]Getting started[/b]
Subscribe and enable Rusted Stellaris in the in-game mod list. For a first game, try Twin Chokepoints and begin with power, mining, alloy production, and research. For multiplayer, everyone should use the same mod version.
